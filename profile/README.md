## How we work

| Stage | What happens |
|---|---|
| **Frame** | Which decision or process is worth changing, what it would take, and what would measurably be different. Short, fixed-price, and yours to keep. |
| **Build** | Working software against your real data, in short fixed-scope increments, in your repositories and on your infrastructure. |
| **Run** | Monitoring that catches a model drifting, defined response times, and a decreasing amount of us. |

Each stage is bought on its own. Most engagements start with the first, and some correctly stop there.
